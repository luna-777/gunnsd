About us

How the team works

Gunn Speech & Debate is student-run. Officers set the practice schedule, teach the novice curriculum, register the team for tournaments, and run practice rounds.

What we do

Debate, speech, drill, compete!

We compete across four branches at local and invitational tournaments throughout the school year. A typical week includes one practice with a short team meeting, branch-specific breakouts, focused drills, and a practice round followed by feedback.
