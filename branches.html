Public Forum

Public Forum is a partner debate event focused on current issues and public policy. Teams use evidence, logic, and clear communication to persuade a judge while responding directly to their opponents’ arguments.

Debate · 2 vs 2

Parliamentary

Parliamentary Debate is a fast-paced, partner debate format that emphasizes quick thinking, logical argumentation, and persuasive speaking. Competitors receive limited preparation time and debate a wide range of topics.

MotionsAnnounced 20 min before
EvidenceNo internet, no prepared cards
Round lengthAbout 45 minutes

Debate · 1 vs 1

Lincoln–Douglas

Lincoln-Douglas is a one-on-one debate event centered on questions of values, ethics, and philosophy. Debaters develop structured cases, analyze competing principles, and defend their reasoning through evidence and argumentation.

TopicsNew every two months
Prep time4 minutes per debater
Round lengthAbout 45 minutes

Speech

Speech events allow competitors to develop their public speaking, interpretation, and performance skills across a variety of formats. Students learn to communicate ideas effectively, connect with an audience, and present with confidence and creativity. Come to practice to learn more!
