Common questions

Before you email.

Do I need experience to join?

No, many people join having never debated!

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes. Come to any Wednesday practice and an officer will catch you up.

Which branch should I pick?

Try a couple. The branches page explains how each round runs.

How much time does it take?

One two-hour practice per week is the typical practice schedule. Tournaments are optional (but highly recommended!) weekend commitments.

Can parents help?

Yes! Tournaments require us to supply judges, and no debate background is needed to be one.
